Size & Volume

How big is it compared to Earth?

Relative Size

  • Diameter: 4,880 km (Slightly larger than Earth's Moon, ~38% of Earth).
  • Volume: Only ~5.4% of Earth's volume.
  • Mass: You could fit about 18 Mercurys inside Earth!
🏋️

Weight & Density

What would you weigh there?

Heavy Metal

  • Density: 5.427 g/cm³. The 2nd densest planet after Earth!
  • Your Weight: Gravity is 38% of Earth's.
  • If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth, you weigh only 38 lbs on Mercury!
🪨

Composition

What is it made of?

A Giant Core

Mercury is fundamentally a terrestrial (rocky) planet, but it's weird inside.

  • Core: A massive metallic core taking up ~85% of the planet's radius.
  • Crust/Mantle: A relatively thin outer shell of silicate rock.
⏱️

Time Constraints

Length of Day & Year

Mind-Bending Time

  • Length of Year: 88 Earth Days (Time to orbit Sun).
  • Length of Day: 59 Earth Days to rotate once.
  • BUT! A full "solar day" (sunrise to sunrise) takes 176 Earth days!
  • A day on Mercury lasts longer than its year!
🌪️

Environment

Moons, Atmosphere & Weather

Harsh Conditions

  • Moons: None. (Zero, Zip, Nada).
  • Atmosphere: Too small and hot to hold one. It has a thin "Exosphere" of blasted atoms.
  • Pressure/Wind: None. Zero atmospheric pressure means no wind or sound.
  • Magnetic Field: Very weak, about 1% the strength of Earth's.
🛰️

Human Exploration

Famous Space Missions

Visiting the Inner World

  • Mariner 10 (1974): First to visit, mapped 45% of surface.
  • MESSENGER (2004-2015): First to orbit. Mapped 100% and discovered ice!
  • BepiColombo (Launched 2018): Joint Euro/Japanese mission, arriving in 2025!
🤯

Wild & Unexpected Facts

Tap to read the weirdest things about Mercury

Truth is Stranger than Fiction

1. It is Shrinking! As its massive iron core cools down, the planet is literally contracting, creating giant cliff-like fault lines called "scarps" on the surface.

2. Fire and Ice: Despite reaching 800°F, deep craters at the poles are in permanent shadows, allowing water ice to exist there!

3. Mercury has a tail! Solar wind blasts sodium atoms off the surface, creating a glowing comet-like tail extending millions of miles behind it.
