Dwarf Planet

Haumea

"The Spinning Football"

A bizarre, fast-spinning dwarf planet in the Kuiper Belt shaped like a football. It hosts a ring system and two small moons, and may be the remnant of an ancient giant collision.

Discovery

Who found it first?

Discovered in 2004

  • Discovered by a Caltech team led by Mike Brown.
  • A Spanish team also announced the discovery shortly after, leading to a contested claim over who found it first.
💫

Fast Spin

A dizzying rotation

3.9 Hour Rotation

  • Haumea completes one full spin in just 3.9 hours, making it the fastest-rotating large object known in the solar system.
  • This extreme spin is what stretched it into its unusual elongated shape.
🏈

Football Shape

Stretched by spin

Ellipsoid Body

  • Centrifugal force from its fast spin stretched Haumea into an ellipsoid measuring 2,322 x 1,704 x 1,138 km.
  • It is one of the most oddly shaped large bodies in the solar system.
💍

Rings

A dwarf with a ring!

Discovered in 2017

  • Astronomers discovered Haumea's ring during a stellar occultation in 2017.
  • Haumea became the first known dwarf planet with rings and only the third body in the outer solar system with them.
🌙

Moons

Two icy companions

Hi'iaka and Namaka

Hi'iaka: The larger and brighter moon, named after the Hawaiian goddess of hula, and thought to be mostly water ice.
Namaka: The smaller, fainter inner moon, named after another daughter of Haumea, and likely made of ice as well.
💥

Collision Family

Remnants of an ancient crash

A Giant Impact

  • Haumea shares its orbit with several smaller objects that have very similar orbits and icy surfaces.
  • Scientists believe these are remnants of a giant impact that blasted pieces off Haumea billions of years ago.
  • This "collisional family" is unique among dwarf planets.
🌌

Orbit

Deep in the Kuiper Belt

43 AU Average Distance

  • Haumea orbits the Sun at an average distance of 43 AU, deep within the Kuiper Belt.
  • One complete orbit takes about 284 years, and its orbit is slightly tilted compared to the planets.
❄️

Surface

Bright crystalline ice

Crystalline Water Ice

  • Haumea's surface is covered in very bright crystalline water ice, which reflects about 80% of the light that hits it.
  • No significant atmosphere has been detected, and the bright ice gives Haumea its high albedo.
