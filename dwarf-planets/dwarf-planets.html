Position & Distance

Where do they hide?

Vast Distances

  • Ceres: ~2.7 AU. The only dwarf in the inner solar system (Asteroid Belt).
  • Pluto: 39 AU. Deep in the Kuiper Belt.
  • Eris: ~68 AU. The furthest known massive object!
📏

Relative Size

Just how small are they?

Smaller than our Moon!

  • Diameter: Pluto is the largest by volume (2,376 km, ~18% of Earth). Ceres is the smallest (~940 km).
  • Massive Rivalry: While Pluto is slightly wider, Eris is actually 27% more massive (heavier) than Pluto!
⚖️

Weight & Density

The Gravity of the Situation

Featherweight Worlds

  • Density: Mostly between 2.0 and 2.5 g/cm³, indicating a mix of rock and ice.
  • Gravity is incredibly weak. On Pluto, gravity is only 0.06x Earth's. If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth, you'd weigh just 6 lbs there!
🧪

Composition

What are they made of?

Ice and Stone

  • Ceres: Rocky core with a thick mantle of water ice.
  • Pluto & Eris: Rock cores wrapped in mantles of water ice, coated in exotic frozen gases like nitrogen and methane ice.
  • Magnetic Field: None have been detected.
⏳

Time Constraints

Length of Day & Century-Long Years

Generations to Orbit

Length of Year: Because they are so far out, their years are incredibly long. Pluto takes 248 years to orbit once. Eris takes a staggering 558 years!
Length of Day: Varies widely. A day on Pluto is 6.4 Earth days. However, Haumea spins so fast its day is only 3.9 hours!
💨

Atmosphere & Weather

Freezing, Collapsing Air

Exotic Climates

  • Pluto: Has a thin atmosphere of nitrogen, methane, and carbon monoxide. As it moves further from the sun, the atmosphere freezes and collapses onto the surface!
  • Eris: Too far and too cold. Its entire atmosphere is currently frozen solid onto its surface.
  • Pressure: Barely there. Pluto's surface pressure is about 1 Pascal (1/100,000th of Earth's).
🛰️

Missions & Moons

Visiting the Far Reaches

The 2015 Milestones

  • Dawn (2015): Arrived in orbit around Ceres, finding mysterious bright salt deposits in its craters.
  • New Horizons (2015): Made a historic flyby of Pluto, revealing towering water-ice mountains and vast nitrogen glaciers.
  • Moons: Pluto has 5 (Charon, Nix, Hydra, Kerberos, Styx). Haumea has 2. Eris and Makemake each have 1. Ceres has zero.
🤯

Wild Facts

The Demotion Drama & Shape Shifters

Weird & Controversial

  • Why Pluto was Demoted: In 2005, Eris was discovered and it was *heavier* than Pluto! Instead of making Eris the 10th planet, astronomers created the "Dwarf Planet" category because neither had cleared their orbital neighborhoods.
  • The Football: Haumea spins so incredibly fast (every 4 hours) that centrifugal force has stretched it out into the shape of a football!
  • Binary System: Charon is so massive compared to Pluto that they actually orbit a point in empty space *between* them!
