Position & Distance

Distance from Sun

The Fifth Planet

  • AU: ~5.2 Astronomical Units
  • KM: ~778 million kilometers
  • Sunlight takes about 43 minutes to travel from the Sun to Jupiter.
📏

Relative Size

The Absolute Behemoth

King of the Planets

  • Diameter: ~139,820 km. That is 11 times wider than Earth!
  • Volume: So large you could fit over 1,300 Earths inside it.
⚖️

Weight & Density

Gravity Check

Heavy but Fluffy

  • Density: 1.33 g/cm³. About a quarter of Earth's density, because it's mostly gas!
  • Gravity is 2.4x Earth's. If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth, you would weigh 240 lbs on Jupiter!
🧪

Composition

Inside the Gas Giant

A Failed Star?

  • Makeup: Mostly Hydrogen (~90%) and Helium (~10%). Similar composition to the Sun.
  • Core: May have a dense, rocky core surrounded by an exotic state of matter called "liquid metallic hydrogen".
  • Magnetism: Its magnetic field is up to 20,000x stronger than Earth's!
⏳

Time constraints

Length of Day & Year

Fast Days, Long Years

Length of Year: 11.8 Earth years. It takes a long time to travel around the sun at that distance.
Length of Day: Only ~9.9 hours! This is the fastest rotating planet in the solar system.
The Bulge: Because it spins so incredibly fast and is made of gas, Jupiter bulges noticeably at its equator (oblate spheroid)!
💨

Atmosphere & Weather

Bands and Giant Storms

Violent Skies

  • No Surface: There is no solid ground. The atmosphere just gets denser and hotter the deeper you go.
  • Bands: The colorful stripes are created by strong jet streams. Dark belts are descending gas; light zones are rising gas.
  • Great Red Spot: A gigantic anticyclonic storm wider than Earth, raging for over 300 years!
🛰️

Famous Missions

Probing the giant

Unlocking the Gas Giant

  • Pioneer 10 (1973): The first spacecraft to fly past Jupiter.
  • Galileo (1995-2003): Orbited Jupiter and dropped a probe into the atmosphere; later intentionally crashed to protect Europa.
  • Juno (2016-Present): Currently in polar orbit mapping its gravitational/magnetic fields.
🤯

Wild Facts

Tap for the strangest Jupiter facts

Cosmic Vacuum Cleaner

  • The Protector: Its massive gravity catches or deflects comets (like Shoemaker-Levy 9 in 1994).
  • Faint Rings: Jupiter has a faint dust ring system created by meteor impacts on its small moons.
  • Auroras: It has massive, permanent auroras driven by its rotation and volcanic material from Io.
