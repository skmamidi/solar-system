King of Moons

How big is it really?

Planet-Sized

  • It is the largest and most massive moon in the solar system.
  • With a radius of 2,634 km, it is actually 8% larger than the planet Mercury!
  • (However, Mercury is made of dense iron, so Mercury is still heavier).
🧲

Magnetic Shield

A unique phenomenon

The Only One

  • Ganymede is the only moon in the solar system known to have an intrinsic magnetic field.
  • It is created by convection within its liquid iron core, working just like Earth's magnetic dynamo!
  • This field creates beautiful glowing auroras at its poles.
🧅

Internal Structure

Layers like an onion

Fully Differentiated

  • Unlike some moons which are just mixed slush, Ganymede is "fully differentiated" (separated into distinct layers).
  • 1. A metallic iron core.
  • 2. A rocky silicate mantle.
  • 3. A thick outer shell of mostly water ice and liquid oceans.
🌗

Surface Terrain

Dark and Light

Two Worlds in One

  • Dark Regions: Very old, highly cratered areas making up about a third of the moon (e.g., Galileo Regio).
  • Light Regions: Younger terrain covered in extensive grooves and ridges called Sulci, likely formed by tectonic faulting.
🌊

The Subsurface Ocean

The "Club Sandwich" Model

Massive Hidden Waters

Scientists believe Ganymede hosts a massive saltwater ocean buried ~150 km beneath its icy crust. It contains more liquid water than all of Earth's oceans combined!
Club Sandwich: Extreme pressure might cause the ocean to exist in stacked layers separated by different phases of exotic, dense ice (Ice III, Ice V).
🎼

Orbital Dance

The Laplace Resonance

The 1:2:4 Harmony

  • Ganymede is locked in a perfect gravitational dance with its sister moons Europa and Io.
  • For every 1 orbit Ganymede makes around Jupiter:
  • Europa makes exactly 2 orbits.
  • Io makes exactly 4 orbits.
  • This "Laplace Resonance" constantly flexes the moons, generating the heat needed to melt their internal oceans!
🛰️

Space Missions

Past and Future

Focus of ESA's JUICE

  • Past: Voyager 1 & 2 mapped it during flybys. The Galileo probe orbited Jupiter and made the shocking discovery of Ganymede's magnetic field.
  • The Future: The European Space Agency (ESA) launched the JUICE (JUpiter Icy Moons Explorer) mission. In 2034, it will become the first spacecraft to ever orbit a moon other than our own!
💨

Atmosphere

Wait, it has air?

A Whisper of Oxygen

  • Yes! Ganymede has an extremely thin atmosphere (an exosphere).
  • It is composed primarily of Oxygen ($O$, $O_2$, and maybe $O_3$ ozone).
  • However, this oxygen isn't from plant life! It's created when Jupiter's intense radiation hits Ganymede's icy surface, splitting the water ($H_2O$) into hydrogen and oxygen.
