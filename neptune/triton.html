Retrograde Orbit

Why is it moving backwards?

The Wrong Way

  • Triton orbits Neptune in a direction opposite to Neptune's rotation.
  • It is the only large moon in the solar system to do this!
  • This "retrograde" orbit strongly proves it wasn't born with Neptune...
🪨

Origins

Where did it come from?

A Captured Dwarf

  • Because of its weird orbit and its similarity to Pluto, scientists are almost certain Triton is actually a Dwarf Planet that drifted too close to Neptune.
  • Neptune's massive gravity "captured" it from the Kuiper Belt millions of years ago!
🍈

Cantaloupe Terrain

A fruity surface

Dimpled Ice

  • The western hemisphere of Triton consists of weird, interconnected depressions and ridges.
  • It looks exactly like the skin of a cantaloupe melon!
  • It was likely formed by "diapirism"—lumps of dirty, slushy ice rising and sinking in the crust over time.
🧊

Composition

What is it made of?

Frozen Nitrogen

  • It has a large rocky/metal core, making up most of its mass.
  • The outer crust is made of water ice, but it is coated in a shiny layer of frozen nitrogen!
  • Its pinkish hue comes from "tholins"—organic compounds created when radiation hits the methane and nitrogen ice.
🌋

Active Geysers

Ice Volcanoes!

Shooting Dust into Space

Solar Heating: Despite being so far from the sun, faint sunlight passes through the clear nitrogen ice, heating dark material underneath.
Eruption: The pressure builds until it violently blasts through the crust, shooting geysers of nitrogen gas and dark dust up to 8 km (5 miles) into the air!
💨

Atmosphere

The delicate winds

Nitrogen Clouds

  • The erupting geysers constantly supply Triton with a very thin atmosphere of Nitrogen (with trace amounts of methane).
  • The atmosphere is extremely thin—only 1/70,000th the pressure of Earth's!
  • Even so, Voyager 2 took pictures of actual, wispy nitrogen clouds floating a few miles above the surface, and winds that carry the geyser dust in long streaks.
🛰️

Famous Missions

The only spacecraft to visit

Voyager 2 (1989)

  • Voyager 2 is the only human object to have ever visited Triton, flying by in 1989.
  • It was Voyager 2 that actually discovered the active geysers! Scientists were shocked to find an active world so far from the sun.
  • Because it only did a flyby, we only have high-res maps of about 40% of the moon.
💥

The Doomed Fate

Spiraling to destruction

Crashing Down

  • Because Triton orbits backwards (retrograde), tidal forces from Neptune are slowly stealing its orbital energy.
  • Triton is slowly spiraling inwards toward Neptune.
  • In about 3.6 billion years, it will cross the Roche limit and be completely torn apart, giving Neptune a ring system more spectacular than Saturn's!
