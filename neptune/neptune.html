Position & Distance

Distance from Sun

The Outer Limit

  • AU: ~30.07 Astronomical Units
  • KM: ~4.5 Billion kilometers
  • It is so far away that sunlight takes 4 hours to reach it!
📏

Relative Size

The Smallest Gas Giant

A Dense Behemoth

  • Diameter: 49,244 km. Nearly 4 times wider than Earth.
  • Volume: You could fit exactly 57 Earths inside Neptune.
⚖️

Weight & Density

Gravity Check

Heavier than Uranus

  • Density: 1.64 g/cm³. It is the densest of all the gas/ice giants.
  • Gravity is 1.14x Earth's! If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth, you'd weigh 114 lbs here.
🧪

Composition

An Ice Giant's Core

Crushing Ice

  • Makeup: A hot, dense fluid of "icy" materials (water, ammonia, methane) situated above a small rocky core.
  • Mantle: The mantle is incredibly hot—often called a "water-ammonia ocean," though it's too dense to be a true liquid.
⏳

Time constraints

Length of Day & Extremely Long Years

A Lifetime Orbit

Length of Year: ~165 Earth years! It recently completed its very first orbit since it was discovered in 1846!
Length of Day: Only 16 hours. Like the other giants, it spins very quickly on its axis.
💨

Atmosphere & Weather

Supersonic Winds and Dark Spots

The Windiest World

  • The Blue Color: Mostly Hydrogen and Helium, but Methane gas gives it its striking, deep blue color.
  • Supersonic Winds: Winds rip through the clouds at over 2,000 km/h (1,200 mph)—faster than the speed of sound on Earth!
  • Great Dark Spot: A massive spinning storm similar to Jupiter's Red Spot, but it frequently vanishes and reappears.
🛰️

Famous Missions

The edge of the solar system

One Brief Visit

  • Voyager 2 (1989): The ONLY spacecraft to ever visit Neptune. It took 12 years to get there!
  • Discoveries: Voyager 2 discovered five moons, four rings, the Great Dark Spot, and active geysers on the moon Triton.
  • Today: We rely entirely on the Hubble and James Webb Space Telescopes to monitor it from Earth.
🤯

Wild Facts

Tap for the strangest Neptune facts

Math and Diamonds

  • Discovered by Math: Neptune is the only planet located via mathematical prediction rather than observation! Astronomers noticed Uranus's orbit was wobbling and calculated another planet must be pulling on it.
  • Diamond Rain: Just like Uranus, extreme pressures deep inside the planet are theorized to compress carbon into diamonds that rain down into the core!
