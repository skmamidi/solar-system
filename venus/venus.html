Venus

"Earth's Evil Twin"

Beautiful from afar, hellish up close. Venus is the hottest planet in the solar system, wrapped in a crushing, toxic atmosphere of carbon dioxide and raining sulfuric acid. Also known as the Morning and Evening Star.

Relative Size

Earth's Twin?

Almost Identical

  • Diameter: ~12,104 km. That is 95% the size of Earth!
  • Volume: Contains about 86% of Earth's volume.

Weight & Density

Gravity Check

Familiar Gravity

  • Density: 5.24 g/cm³. Very similar to Earth's rock and metal makeup.
  • If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth, you'd weigh ~90 lbs on Venus!
🪨

Composition

Inside the planet

Rocky World

  • Just like Earth, it has a central iron core and a rocky mantle.
  • Its surface is covered in volcanic rock. There are thousands of volcanoes, some of which may still be active today!
⏳

Time constraints

Length of Day & Year (It gets weird)

A Day Longer Than A Year

Length of Year: 225 Earth Days to orbit the sun once.
Length of Day: 243 Earth Days to rotate once on its axis.
Retrograde Rotation: Venus spins backwards compared to most planets. The Sun rises in the West and sets in the East!
🌧️

Atmosphere & Weather

A toxic, crushing hellscape

Runaway Greenhouse

  • Air: 96% Carbon Dioxide (CO2), creating a severe greenhouse effect that traps heat.
  • Clouds: Thick, yellowish clouds made of toxic Sulfuric Acid.
  • Pressure: 92 times greater than Earth. Walking on the surface would feel like being 1 kilometer underwater!
  • Wind: High altitude clouds whip around at 360 km/h.
🛰️

Missions & Moons

Who has visited? Who lives there?

Exploration

  • Moons: None. Venus and Mercury are the only planets without moons.
  • Venera Program (Soviets): In the 1970s/80s, they landed probes on the surface. They survived only a few hours before being crushed/melted.
  • Magellan (NASA): Mapped 98% of the surface using radar through the thick clouds in the 1990s.
  • Upcoming: NASA's DAVINCI and VERITAS missions are planned for the early 2030s.
🤯

Wild Facts

Tap for the strangest Venus facts

Unexpected Truths

  • No Magnetic Shield: Despite having an iron core, its extremely slow rotation means it doesn't generate a strong magnetic field like Earth does.
  • Metal Snow: High atop Venus's mountains, it is believed to "snow" metal (galena and bismuthinite) because the surface is so hot that metals vaporize and condense at higher altitudes!
