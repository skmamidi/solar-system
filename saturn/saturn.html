Position & Distance

Distance from Sun

The Outer Giant

  • AU: ~9.5 Astronomical Units
  • KM: ~1.4 Billion kilometers
  • Sunlight takes about 80 minutes to reach Saturn!
📏

Relative Size

The Second Largest

Massive Proportions

  • Diameter: ~116,460 km. About 9 times wider than Earth.
  • Volume: You could fit exactly 764 Earths inside it.
⚖️

Weight & Density

The Fluffy Planet

Lighter Than Water

  • Density: 0.687 g/cm³. It is the ONLY planet less dense than water. It would float in a giant bathtub!
  • Gravity is 1.06x Earth's. If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth, you'd weigh 106 lbs here!
🧪

Composition

Gas Giant Anatomy

Hydrogen World

  • Makeup: Primarily Hydrogen (96%) and Helium (3%).
  • Core: Likely a dense core of rock, ice, and liquid metallic hydrogen, subjected to intense pressure.
  • Surface: No solid surface to stand on.
⏳

Time constraints

Length of Day & Year

Fast Spin, Slow Orbit

Length of Year: 29.4 Earth years. A very long journey around the sun.
Length of Day: Only 10.7 hours! It spins so fast that it visibly flattens at the poles.
It took years for scientists to figure out the day length because gas giants don't have solid surface features to track! They had to use radio emissions.
💿

The Rings

What are they made of?

Billions of Snowballs

  • Composition: Not solid rings! They are made of billions of pieces of water ice, dust, and rock.
  • Size: They extend up to 282,000 km from the planet, but are incredibly thin—mostly only 10 meters thick!
  • Shepherd Moons: Tiny moons live *inside* the gaps of the rings, using their gravity to keep the ring particles in neat lines.
🛰️

Famous Missions

Exploring the Ringed Giant

The Grand Tour

  • Pioneer 11 (1979): The first spacecraft to fly by and study the rings up close.
  • Voyager 1 & 2 (1980-81): Revealed the rings are made of thousands of smaller ringlets.
  • Cassini-Huygens (2004-2017): Orbited Saturn for 13 years, took breathtaking photos, and actually dropped a probe (Huygens) onto the moon Titan!
🤯

Wild Facts

Tap for the strangest Saturn facts

Geometry in Space

  • The Hexagon: Saturn has a massive, persistent storm at its North Pole that is shaped like a perfect hexagon. It is wider than Earth!
  • Disappearing Rings: Because Saturn is tilted, every 14-15 years we view its rings completely edge-on from Earth, making them seem to magically disappear!
  • Diamond Rain: Lightning storms turn methane gas into soot, which compresses into diamonds as it falls deep into the atmosphere!
