Absolute Scale

How massive is it?

The Giant

  • Diameter: 1.39 Million km.
  • Volume: You could fit exactly 1.3 Million Earths inside the Sun!
🧪

Composition

What is it made of?

Plasma Ball

  • The Sun is not solid or gas, it is plasma.
  • It is roughly 73% Hydrogen ($H$) and 25% Helium ($He$).
  • The remaining 2% consists of heavier elements like Oxygen, Carbon, and Iron.
⚖️

Gravity & Weight

Crushing forces

The Anchor

  • Surface Gravity: 27.9 times that of Earth.
  • If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth, you would weigh 2,790 lbs on the Sun! (Assuming you didn't burn up instantly).
🔄

Rotation

Differential Spin

Differential Rotation

  • Because the Sun isn't solid, different parts spin at different speeds!
  • Equator: Takes 25 days to rotate once.
  • Poles: Take up to 36 days to rotate once.
🧲

Magnetic Tangle

The Solar Cycle

The 11-Year Cycle

Because the equator spins faster than the poles, the Sun's magnetic field gets violently twisted and tangled up over time.
Solar Maximum: Every 11 years, the tangles snap, creating tons of sunspots and solar flares.
The Flip: At the peak of this 11-year cycle, the Sun's North and South magnetic poles literally flip places!
🌌

The Heliosphere

The Sun's Reach

Our Protective Bubble

  • The Sun constantly blows out a stream of charged particles called the Solar Wind.
  • This wind travels far past Pluto and the Kuiper Belt, creating a giant magnetic bubble called the Heliosphere.
  • This bubble shields our entire solar system from deadly cosmic radiation coming from the rest of the galaxy! Voyager 1 and 2 just recently crossed this boundary into interstellar space.
🛰️

Famous Missions

Touching the Sun

Touching the Star

  • SOHO & SDO: Satellites constantly staring at the Sun from Earth's orbit, providing us with real-time space weather alerts.
  • Parker Solar Probe (Launched 2018): The fastest human-made object ever! It is currently "touching the Sun" by flying directly through its outer atmosphere (the Corona) to study why it is hotter than the surface.
🤯

Wild Facts

Tap for strange solar facts

Paradoxes & Energy

  • The Temperature Paradox: The surface of the Sun is about 5,500°C, but its outer atmosphere (the Corona) is inexplicably hotter at over 1 Million °C!
  • Ancient Light: It takes a photon of light up to 100,000 years to bounce its way out of the dense core of the Sun. Once it reaches the surface, it takes only 8 minutes to reach Earth!
