Position & Distance

Distance from Sun

The Deep Freeze

  • KM: ~2.9 Billion kilometers
  • Sunlight takes 2 hours and 40 minutes to reach Uranus!

Relative Size

The Third Largest

A Giant of Ice

  • Diameter: 51,118 km. About 4 times wider than Earth.
  • Volume: You could fit about 63 Earths inside it.
⚖️

Weight & Density

Surprisingly Light Gravity

Less Pull Than Earth

  • Density: 1.27 g/cm³. The second least dense planet after Saturn.
  • Gravity is only 89% of Earth's! If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth, you'd weigh 89 lbs here.
🧪

Composition

What makes an Ice Giant?

Icy Slush

  • Makeup: Unlike Jupiter/Saturn, it's mostly a hot, dense fluid of "icy" materials: water, methane, and ammonia.
  • Core: A small, rocky core at the center.
  • Surface: No solid surface to stand on.
⏳

Time constraints

Length of Day & Extreme Seasons

Generational Seasons

Length of Year: 84 Earth years to orbit the sun once.
Length of Day: 17 hours and 14 minutes.
Because of its severe 98° tilt, the poles get 42 years of continuous sunlight, followed by 42 years of complete darkness!
💨

Atmosphere & Weather

Why is it blue? What does it smell like?

A Smelly Blue World

  • The Blue Color: Methane gas in the upper atmosphere absorbs red light and reflects blue light.
  • The Smell: Recent studies found hydrogen sulfide in the cloud tops. If you could breathe it, it would smell like rotten eggs!
  • Temperature: It is the coldest planetary atmosphere, even colder than Neptune!
🛰️

Famous Missions

Exploring the Outer Reaches

The Lone Visitor

  • Voyager 2 (1986): The ONLY spacecraft to ever visit Uranus.
  • Discoveries: It discovered 10 new moons, 2 new rings, and a strangely off-center magnetic field during its brief flyby.
  • Future: NASA and ESA are currently designing future orbiter missions to finally return!
🤯

Wild Facts

Tap for the strangest Uranus facts

Rolling and Raining Gems

  • The Bowling Ball: Unlike any other planet, its equator is nearly at a right angle to its orbit (97.77° tilt), meaning it rolls around the sun like a ball!
  • Diamond Rain: Deep in the mantle, extreme pressure and temperature squeeze carbon atoms out of methane, causing solid diamonds to sink towards the core!
  • Lopsided Magnet: The magnetic field isn't in the center of the planet—it's shifted away from the core and tilted wildly.
