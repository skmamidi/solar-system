"The Brightest Uranian"

Discovery

Who found it?

William Lassell

  • Discovered in 1851 by William Lassell (the same guy who discovered Triton!).
  • It orbits ~190,000 km from Uranus, taking just 2.5 Earth days to complete one orbit.
✨

Albedo

The brightest of its kind

Reflecting Light

  • Ariel has the highest albedo (reflectivity) of all Uranian moons.
  • It reflects about 39% of the sunlight that hits it, suggesting a very fresh, clean icy surface compared to its darker sibling Umbriel.
📖

Naming

A break from mythology

Literature Spirits

  • Unlike most moons named after Greek/Roman gods, moons of Uranus are named after characters from classic English literature.
  • "Ariel" is a sylph (air spirit) in Alexander Pope's The Rape of the Lock and also a spirit in Shakespeare's The Tempest.
🪨

Composition

What is it made of?

Half Rock, Half Ice

  • Ariel is believed to consist of roughly equal parts water ice and dense rock.
  • Scientists believe it may be differentiated—meaning the heavy rock sank to form a core, surrounded by an icy mantle.
☀️

Extreme Seasons

The side effects of a sideways planet

42-Year Winters

Because Uranus rolls on its side (98° tilt), its moons orbit along its equator perpendicular to the sun.
This means Ariel's poles spend 42 Earth years in continuous sunlight, followed by 42 Earth years of total, freezing darkness!
🌋

Chasmata & Cryovolcanism

The most distinct surface feature

Rift Valleys of Ice

  • Ariel's surface is crisscrossed by an extensive network of grabens (deep, steep-sided rift valleys or canyons) called Chasmata.
  • The floors of these canyons are incredibly smooth, showing very few craters.
  • Scientists believe this is proof of past cryovolcanism (ice volcanoes)—warm, slushy liquid water/ammonia erupted from inside the moon, filling the valleys and freezing over into fresh, smooth ice!
🛰️

Voyager 2

The only visitor

A Brief Encounter

  • The NASA Voyager 2 probe is the only spacecraft to have ever visited Ariel, making a flyby in January 1986.
  • Because of Ariel's extreme seasons, the northern hemisphere was pitched into total darkness. Voyager 2 was only able to photograph and map about 35% of the moon's surface!
🕳️

Kachina Chasmata

The great canyon

A Massive Rift

  • The longest known canyon system on Ariel is the Kachina Chasmata.
  • It is over 620 km long and up to 50 km wide!
  • Like all features on Ariel, it is named after benevolent spirits from global mythology (Kachina refers to spirits in Pueblo culture).
