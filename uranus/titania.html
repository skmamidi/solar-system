Discovery & Naming

Queen of the Fairies

William Herschel

  • Discovered in 1787 by William Herschel, on the very same day he discovered Oberon!
  • It is named after Titania, the mythical Queen of the Fairies in William Shakespeare's play A Midsummer Night's Dream.
📏

Size & Scale

How big is it?

The Largest Uranian

  • Titania is the largest of Uranus's 28 known moons.
  • At 1,577 km in diameter, it ranks as the 8th largest moon in the entire solar system.
  • It is slightly smaller than Earth's moon (about 45% the size).
🧊

Composition

What is inside?

Differentiated Iceball

  • It consists of roughly equal parts water ice and dense rock.
  • Scientists believe it is fully differentiated, meaning the heavy rock sank to form a solid rocky core, surrounded by an icy mantle and crust.
⚡

Messina Chasma

The giant scar

A Massive Canyon

  • Titania is scarred by an enormous system of fault lines and canyons.
  • The largest is Messina Chasma. It is nearly 1,500 kilometers long—long enough to stretch from New York to Florida!
🪨

Surface Evolution

Cracking the crust

Expansion Tectonics

Craters: Like Oberon, the surface is heavily cratered. However, there are very few massive impact basins, suggesting the surface was resurfaced early in its history.
The Cracks: The massive canyons likely formed when Titania's interior cooled and expanded, literally bursting the outer icy crust open!
❄️

Extreme Seasons

A long, dark winter

Sideways Orbit

  • Because Uranus is tilted 98 degrees on its side, its moons orbit in its equatorial plane.
  • This means Titania's poles experience extreme seasons. The north pole will see 42 Earth years of continuous sunlight, followed by 42 Earth years of complete, freezing darkness!
🛰️

Voyager 2 Flyby

The only close look

A Half-Seen World

  • NASA's Voyager 2 is the only spacecraft to have ever visited Titania, passing by in January 1986 at a distance of 365,200 km.
  • Because of the 42-year seasons, the northern hemisphere was pitched in total darkness. Voyager 2 could only map about 40% of the moon's surface!
💨

Atmosphere

Does it have air?

A Seasonal Whisper

  • While mostly a vacuum, scientists suspect Titania might have an incredibly thin, transient atmosphere of Carbon Dioxide (CO₂).
  • During its 42-year summer, the sun might heat the ice just enough to sublimate some CO₂ into a faint gas, which immediately freezes back to the ground when winter returns!
