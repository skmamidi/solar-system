Discovery & Naming

The Melancholy Sprite

William Lassell

  • Discovered in 1851 by William Lassell, alongside Ariel.
  • Because it was so much darker than Ariel, it was named after Umbriel, the "dusky melancholy sprite" from Alexander Pope's poem The Rape of the Lock.
🌑

Albedo

Why so dark?

Absorbing Light

  • Umbriel is the darkest of Uranus's major moons.
  • It reflects only 21% of the sunlight that hits it (its sister Ariel reflects 39%).
  • Because it absorbs more sunlight, it is slightly warmer than the other moons!
⭕

Wunda Crater

The bright anomaly

The Glowing Ring

  • The most famous feature on the dark moon is a glaringly bright ring resting on its equator.
  • It is a massive crater named Wunda, measuring 131 km across. The bright ring is likely caused by a fresh layer of highly reflective frost or ice!
☄️

Ancient Surface

Geologically dead

Heavy Bombardment

  • Unlike Ariel, Umbriel shows almost zero signs of tectonic activity or recent ice volcanoes.
  • It is the most heavily cratered of the Uranian moons, meaning its surface has been "dead" and unchanging for billions of years.
🧊

Composition

What is beneath the dark dust?

A Frozen Slushy

Ice & Rock: It is composed of roughly equal amounts of water ice and dense rock.
Undifferentiated: Unlike Titania, Umbriel's interior may not be cleanly separated into a core and mantle. The rock and ice are likely mixed uniformly together!
❄️

Extreme Seasons

The side effects of a sideways planet

42-Year Winters

Because Uranus rolls on its side (98° tilt), its moons orbit along its equator perpendicular to the sun.
This means Umbriel's poles spend 42 Earth years in continuous sunlight, followed by 42 Earth years of total, freezing darkness!
🛰️

Voyager 2

The only visitor

A Brief Encounter

  • NASA's Voyager 2 is the only spacecraft to have ever visited Umbriel, making a brief flyby in January 1986.
  • It passed by at a distance of 325,000 km. Because the northern hemisphere was pitched in a 42-year winter, Voyager 2 could only map about 40% of the moon's surface!
🕳️

Craters of Darkness

What else is on the surface?

Goblin Names

  • Besides Wunda, the moon features other massive impact craters, but they lack the bright frost rays seen on other moons.
  • Keeping with the "dark sprite" theme, all geological features on Umbriel are named after dark or evil spirits from mythology! (e.g., Malingee, Skynd).
