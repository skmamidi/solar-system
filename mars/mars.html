Position & Distance

Distance from Sun

The Fourth Planet

  • AU: ~1.52 Astronomical Units
  • KM: ~228 million kilometers
  • Light takes about 13 minutes to travel from the Sun to Mars.

Relative Size

Earth's Little Brother

Smaller Than You Think

  • Diameter: 6,792 km. That is only about 53% the size of Earth!
  • Volume: Contains only 15% of Earth's volume.
⚖️

Weight & Density

Gravity Check

Low Gravity Environment

  • Density: 3.93 g/cm³. Much less dense than Earth.
  • If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth, you only weigh 38 lbs on Mars! (Exactly the same surface gravity as Mercury!)
🪨

Composition

Why is it red?

The Rust Planet

  • Core: Dense core of iron, nickel, and sulfur.
  • Surface: Covered in iron oxide dust. Yes, the planet is literally covered in rust!
  • Magnetic Field: None! It lost its global magnetic shield billions of years ago.
⏳

Time constraints

Length of Day & Year

A Familiar Schedule

Length of Year: 687 Earth days. Almost twice as long as an Earth year.
Length of Day: 24 hours and 37 minutes. A Martian day is called a "Sol".
Seasons: Mars has an axial tilt of 25 degrees (very similar to Earth's 23.5), meaning it has distinct seasons, though they last twice as long!
💨

Atmosphere & Weather

Dust storms and thin air

A Faint Whisper

  • Air: 95% Carbon Dioxide (CO2). It is over 100 times thinner than Earth's atmosphere.
  • Weather: Despite the thin air, it can support massive wind storms. Sometimes, global dust storms cover the entire planet for months!
  • Water: Cannot exist as a liquid on the surface due to low pressure, but there is water ice at the poles and underground.
🤖

Famous Missions

A planet inhabited solely by robots

The Robotic Vanguard

  • Viking 1 & 2 (1976): The first spacecraft to successfully land and operate on the Martian surface.
  • Rovers: Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity, and Perseverance have all driven across Mars.
  • Ingenuity (2021): The first helicopter to perform powered, controlled flight on another planet!
🤯

Wild Facts

Tap for the strangest Martian facts

Extreme Geography

  • Olympus Mons: The largest volcano in the solar system. It is 3 times taller than Mount Everest and the size of the state of Arizona!
  • Valles Marineris: A grand canyon that is 4,000 km long and up to 7 km deep. It would stretch entirely across the United States.
  • Blue Sunsets: Because of the fine dust in the atmosphere, sunsets on Mars appear a cool, haunting blue.
