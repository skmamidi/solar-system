Position & Distance

Distance from Sun

The Goldilocks Zone

  • AU: Exactly 1.0 Astronomical Unit (we define the AU!)
  • KM: ~149.6 million kilometers
  • The perfect distance for liquid water to exist.
📏

Relative Size

The Baseline Metric

Largest Rocky Planet

  • Diameter: 12,742 km. It is an "oblate spheroid", meaning it bulges slightly at the equator!
  • Volume: 1 Earth Volume. (We use Earth to measure the size of exoplanets!)
⚖️

Weight & Density

Gravity Check

The Densest Planet

  • Density: 5.51 g/cm³. The densest planet in the Solar System!
  • If you weigh 100 lbs on Earth... well, you weigh exactly 100 lbs. Because you are on Earth.
🧲

Composition

Inside the planet

Magnetic Dynamo

  • Core: Solid inner iron core, liquid outer core.
  • Crust: Divided into constantly moving tectonic plates.
  • Magnetosphere: The spinning liquid core creates a strong magnetic shield protecting us from solar radiation.
⏳

Time constraints

Length of Day & Year

Precision Timing

Length of Year: 365.24 days. That extra 0.24 is why we have Leap Years!
Length of Day: Exactly 23 hours, 56 minutes, and 4 seconds (a sidereal day).
The Tilt: Earth is tilted on its axis at 23.5 degrees, which is the sole reason we experience seasons!
🌱

Atmosphere & Life

The Breathable Blanket

A Perfect Balance

  • Air: 78% Nitrogen, 21% Oxygen, 1% other (Argon, CO2).
  • Water: 71% of the surface is covered in oceans. It is the only place in the universe known to have liquid water on its surface.
  • Biosphere: Estimated to contain 8.7 million species of life, supported entirely by the atmospheric oxygen and water cycle.
🛰️

Missions & Moons

Satellites and Luna

The Crowded Orbit

  • Moons: One (Luna). It stabilizes our axial tilt and creates the ocean tides.
  • Exploration: Instead of sending probes *to* Earth, we send thousands of satellites *around* it to monitor weather, climate, and communications.
  • Notable Missions: The International Space Station (ISS) orbits Earth 16 times a day with a permanent human crew!
🤯

Wild Facts

Tap for the strangest Earth facts

Unexpected Truths

  • The Name: Earth is the ONLY planet in our solar system not named after a Greek or Roman deity. The name comes from the Old English and Germanic words meaning "the ground".
  • Gravity Variations: Because Earth is bumpy and unevenly dense, gravity actually varies slightly depending on where you stand (like in Hudson Bay, Canada, where gravity is slightly lower)!
